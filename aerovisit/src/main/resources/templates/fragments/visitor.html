Data Privacy Act of 2012 · R.A. 10173

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

The PAF Aerospace Museum, Villamor Air Base, Pasay City, is the personal information controller for this Visitor Management Portal. Because the museum is located inside an active military installation, we collect only the information needed to verify your identity, keep the base secure and manage your visit.

1. Information we collect

  • Booking details: full name, sex, age, address, affiliation, visitor type, contact number, email address, visit date and time window, purpose of visit, group size and the names of your companions.
  • Identity photos: a close-up photo of your valid ID and a selfie of you holding it.
  • Biometric data: a face template (a set of numbers computed from the face in your selfie), used only for identity verification and base security.
  • Entry records: your booking reference, gate check-in and check-out times, and the personnel who processed your entry.
  • Feedback: ratings, comments and the museum area you choose to review.
  • CCTV images: the gates, museum grounds and exhibit halls are monitored by closed-circuit television.

2. Why we process it

  • To screen and verify visitors before they are allowed onto a military installation.
  • To manage bookings, daily visitor capacity and entry at the gate, including your QR entry pass.
  • To send you booking confirmations, reminders and notices about schedule changes or closures.
  • To investigate security incidents and comply with lawful orders.
  • To produce visitor statistics and improve the museum, using feedback and attendance data.

Your photos are checked by software on museum servers: text recognition reads your name from your ID, and face matching compares the photo on your ID with your selfie to confirm that you are the ID holder (one-to-one verification). Your face template is also compared with faces barred from the base for security violations, and with other bookings for the same date so that one person holds only one booking per day. This biometric processing takes place only on museum servers; your photos and face template are never sent to any outside service. These checks only assist our staff: every booking is reviewed and approved by authorized museum personnel before an entry pass is issued.

3. Sharing and disclosure

We do not sell, rent or share your personal data with third parties for marketing or for any purpose unrelated to your visit. Your information is disclosed only:

  • to Philippine Air Force security units and law enforcement agencies when required for base security, or when required by law; and
  • to service providers that operate parts of this portal on our behalf: Google email services (booking emails and email verification codes) and Google Gemini (the AI chat assistant, which receives the questions you type into it). Please do not type personal information into the chat assistant.

4. Retention

  • CCTV footage is kept for up to 60 days, unless it is needed for an ongoing security investigation.
  • ID photos and face templates are processed temporarily and deleted automatically 30 days after the visit date, once the visit is completed, missed, rejected, cancelled or denied entry at the gate. They are kept longer only when the face is barred from the base for a security violation.
  • Booking, entry and feedback records are kept only as long as necessary for base security and visitor statistics, and are then securely disposed of in line with National Privacy Commission rules and government records-retention policies.

5. How we protect it

Visitor records are accessible only to authorized museum personnel through role-based accounts with hashed passwords. Personnel handle personal data on a need-to-know basis and are bound by confidentiality.

6. Your rights

Under R.A. 10173 you have the right to be informed, to access your data, to object to processing, to have inaccurate data corrected, to have your data erased or blocked when processing is no longer lawful, to data portability, to claim damages, and to file a complaint with the National Privacy Commission (privacy.gov.ph).

To exercise these rights, contact the museum's Data Protection Officer at museum@example.com or through the museum administration office. You can review or cancel your own booking at any time on the Manage Booking page.

Villamor Air Base · Security Regulations

Terms of Entry & Base Regulations

Last updated: October 2026

The PAF Aerospace Museum is inside Villamor Air Base, an active military installation. By booking a visit you agree to the following terms. Base security personnel enforce them, and their instructions must be followed at all times.

1. Approved booking and entry pass

Only visitors with an approved booking are admitted. Present your QR entry pass or Booking Reference ID at the gate on your scheduled date and time window. Bookings cannot be transferred to another person.

2. Valid identification

Every visitor must present a valid government-issued photo ID at the gate. The booking holder must present the same ID uploaded during registration, and companions must match the names listed in the group manifest. Minors must be accompanied by a responsible adult.

3. Inspection of persons, bags and vehicles

All persons, bags and vehicles entering the base are subject to inspection. Refusing an inspection is grounds for denial of entry. Large bags and backpacks are not allowed inside the museum.

4. Prohibited items

  • Firearms, ammunition, explosives, bladed weapons and any item that can be used as a weapon.
  • Flammable materials, illegal drugs and alcoholic beverages.
  • Drones and other unmanned aerial devices.
  • Food and drinks inside the exhibit halls.

5. Restricted zones and photography

Stay within the museum, the aircraft park and other areas open to visitors. Operational areas, flight lines and any area marked "Restricted" are strictly off-limits. Photography is allowed inside the museum and aircraft park, but not of checkpoints, base installations, security personnel or aircraft operations outside the museum area.

6. Dress code and conduct

  • Wear decent, appropriate attire. Sleeveless shirts, short shorts, torn clothing and slippers may be refused at the gate.
  • Smoking and vaping are not allowed anywhere on the museum grounds.
  • Do not touch, climb or enter any aircraft or exhibit unless museum staff allow it. Children must be supervised at all times.
  • Visitors are liable for any damage they cause to exhibits or base property.

7. Right to refuse entry

Base security may deny entry to, or escort out, anyone who breaks these regulations or poses a security risk. The museum may cancel bookings or close without notice for security, operational or emergency reasons; affected visitors are notified by email.
