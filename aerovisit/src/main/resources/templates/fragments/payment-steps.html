How to pay on LANDBANK

  1. Open the LANDBANK payment page (button below) and click "Pay Now".
  2. In the merchant search, type exactly: PHILIPPINE AIR FORCE (all capital letters, with one space between each word), select it and click "Continue".
  3. Choose the transaction: PAF Aerospace Museum, then click "Continue".
  4. Type your billing number exactly as shown. A wrong number can delay your booking. Enter your billing number exactly: 000123, and the exact amount: PHP 650.00.
  5. Complete the payment (GCash, card or bank), then take a screenshot of the Landbank confirmation page that shows your billing number and amount, and upload it below.
