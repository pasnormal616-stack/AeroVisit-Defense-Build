Bookings & Visits

Advance Bookings

With 1, the earliest advance date is tomorrow (Manila time). Today is Walk-in only. Bookings already made are not changed.

Walk-ins

People already at the museum can book for today with a photo of their ID; the guard checks their ID and receipt at the gate. An unpaid walk-in is released after the minutes below (never later than the end of its time window); a free walk-in must check in within them.

Feedback Email

Every visitor who was checked in gets one feedback email this many minutes after their booked time window ends (or at check-out, if that comes first). Visitors still shown as inside are not checked out by it.

Prices & Payment

Admission Prices

Shown on the public booking page and used to price new bookings. Bookings already made keep the prices they were made at.

Active Military / AFP CivHR / Dependents of Active Military are always free.

Payment

Visitors pay on LANDBANK Link.BizPortal and upload their receipt. Unpaid bookings release their seats at the deadline: the earlier of the hours below after approval and the start of the booked time window.

Must start with https://. Leave it empty to hide the "Open LANDBANK Payment Page" button; visitors then see the museum contact email instead.
